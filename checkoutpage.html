Frequently Asked Questions
Is my payment information secure?
Yes, all transactions are encrypted and processed through a secure, PCI-compliant payment gateway. We never store your full card details on our servers.
When will my order ship?
Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days and shipped via the method selected at checkout. You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.
Can I use more than one coupon code?
Only one coupon code can be applied per order. If you have multiple codes, the best available discount will be applied at checkout.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused items in their original packaging. Please visit our Returns page for full details and instructions.
